# Winnie — Capabilities Annex (v1.0)

**Purpose:** This document is an appendix to MASTER_SPEC.md. The master
spec describes Winnie's safety architecture — auth, queue, AI guardrails,
audit log. This annex describes what Winnie actually *does* day-to-day.

If MASTER_SPEC is the constitution, this is the job description.

---

## 1. Personality

**Voice:** British, sassy, fun, *genius level*. Reference: Elizabeth
Hurley's character in *Bedazzled*. Cool, dry, slightly arch. Confident,
not formal. Knows everything, holds it lightly. Never sycophantic, never
robotic.

**Behaviour:**
- Always there (persistent UI, sidebar or corner of screen)
- Voice listening is **opt-in** — push-to-talk button or hot-mic with a
  visible mute toggle. Default: muted. Aaron presses to talk or unmutes
  when he wants her listening.
- She narrates rather than chats. Concise. Doesn't ask "anything else?"
- Calm but watchful. When something needs Aaron's attention, she
  surfaces it. When nothing does, she doesn't fill silence.
- Dry humour permitted. Self-aware, never overplayed.

**She does not:**
- Pretend to be human
- Use sycophancy ("Great question!")
- Ask permission for things she's been told to do, but always shows
  what she's about to do
- Act on consequential things without queue approval

## 2. Aaron's working day with Winnie

### Morning brief (8 am)
Pre-generated by Winnie overnight. On Aaron's screen when he sits down.
Contains:
- Anything urgent that came in overnight (flagged emails, deadlines)
- Today's calendar at a glance
- Pending applications waiting for approval (CBA + SEC)
- Hardship Fund items awaiting trustee response
- Consultations with deadlines this week
- Any actions in the queue from yesterday that Aaron didn't get to
- A short summary: "Three things matter most today: X, Y, Z"

### During the day
- Voice or text commands at any time
- Watches all 28 mailboxes (CBA + SEC)
- Drafts replies for review (never sends without approval, except for
  pre-defined auto-confirmation templates like "we've received your
  application")
- Files everything into the right NAS folder automatically
- Logs every action

### Evening wind-down
- Aaron tells her what he didn't get to
- She rolls items forward to tomorrow's brief
- She queues anything that needs to happen overnight (e.g. send the
  Tuesday newsletter at 7 am Wednesday)
- She powers down with the UPS if power's lost

### Mailbox processing

For every email arriving in any mailbox, Winnie:

1. **Classifies** — sender, organisation, topic, urgency
2. **Routes** — to the right NAS folder + flags any required action
3. **Drafts** if a reply is appropriate (queued for Aaron's approval)
4. **Files** the email itself into the NAS structure
5. **Logs** every action to the audit log

**Time-critical handling:** Aaron's correspondents typically mark urgent
items with "URGENT" or "EMERGENCY" in the subject. These get pushed to
the top of Aaron's morning brief AND a real-time notification, regardless
of which mailbox they arrive in.

**Auto-replies (sendable without approval):**
- Application receipt confirmations ("we have received your application,
  we will respond within 7 working days")
- Out-of-office during Aaron's holidays
- Generic acknowledgements (only from explicitly approved templates)

**Everything else** drafts → queue → Aaron approves → send.

The NAS is the source of truth for all files. Winnie reads/writes to it

### Remote access via Cloudflare Tunnel

Each committee gets read access to ONLY their own folder via a URL
like `cba.criminalbar.com/files/wellbeing/`. Authentication via
Cloudflare Access (SSO + MFA). They cannot see other committees'
folders. Aaron sees everything.

**File changes propagate instantly** — when Aaron drops a file into
`Z:\2026\03-Committees\Wellbeing\` locally, the wellbeing committee
sees it immediately on the website. No sync delay (it's the same file
served via Tunnel).

## 5. Membership lifecycle (CBA + SEC)

### Application flow

1. **Applicant fills form** on website (criminalbar.com or
   southeastcircuit.org.uk)
2. **Auto-confirmation** sent immediately: "We've received your
   application, we will respond within 7 working days. (It usually
   happens within hours.)"
3. **Application appears in Aaron's queue** as a PendingAction
4. **Aaron approves** — usually within hours. Special case: applicants
   who declare a chambers email and tick "I am a barrister at a
   participating chambers scheme" are **auto-approvable** subject to
   Aaron's one-click confirm. (No automatic approval without his click.)
5. **On approval:**
   - Member record created in the CBA app's database (or SEC's)
   - Membership ID generated
   - Account login emailed to applicant: "Welcome — here's your app
     login, here's your website login, here's a tour of what's
     available"
   - If chambers-scheme: no payment action (chambers pays annually)
   - If individual: GoCardless link emailed for direct debit setup

### Annual chambers run

Once a year, Winnie:
1. Generates the per-chambers list of CBA / SEC members
2. Sends each chambers contact a confirmation email with their list
3. Chambers updates: removes leavers, adds joiners
4. Updated list comes back; Winnie reconciles
5. Removed members: account deactivated (data retained for X years per
   GDPR retention policy)
6. Added members: account created automatically + welcome email sent

### Existing 1,800 barristers with direct-debit mandates

When GoCardless is set up, Aaron has 1,800 existing barristers with
their bank details on file (currently held in [Sage spreadsheet —
to confirm]). Winnie's first migration job:

1. Import the existing records
2. Create GoCardless mandates for each (this requires the existing
   mandate to be in a transferrable format — to verify with GoCardless
   support)
3. Reconcile against active CBA/SEC membership lists
4. Generate Aaron a "ready to migrate" report for approval

## 6. The Hardship Fund

**Status:** Charity with charitable status, separate governance from
the CBA itself.

**Architecture:** Hardship sits *beside* the CBA app, not inside it.
Different access controls. Different audit log.

**Roles:**
- **Aaron + Winnie** — intake. Reads applications, summarises for the
  panel.
- **Trustee panel** — decides outcomes. Each trustee logs in with their
  own credentials, sees only Hardship-relevant data.

**Application flow:**
1. Applicant submits hardship form (on CBA website)
2. Application visible to Aaron + Winnie only
3. Aaron forwards (via Winnie) to trustee panel as CC
4. AI summary attached (composition of the case, no medical/financial
   speculation)
5. Trustee panel discusses, decides, logs decision
6. Outcome communicated to applicant

**Data stored:**
- Applicant name (no anonymous ID — tied to barrister)
- Case summary (free text)
- Supporting docs (uploaded to NAS, restricted access)
- Decision + rationale + amount + date
- Audit log of every read/write

**AI permitted:**
- Summarising case for trustee briefing
- Drafting decision letters for trustee review

**AI prohibited:**
- Making decisions
- Suggesting outcomes
- Anything that touches financial advice

## 12. The Big Ingestion (Phase X — after core build)

After Winnie + the four core apps are live, Winnie has a multi-week
catch-up job:

### 12a. Email ingestion
- Read all CBA + SEC emails back to start of records
- Read Aaron's personal email back to the 1990s
- Classify every email
- File into NAS by year/function/correspondent
- Build a searchable index
- Result: institutional memory becomes searchable in seconds

### 12b. File system scan
- Crawl Aaron's tower (desktop, Documents, Downloads, etc.)
- Identify temporary/cache/orphan files
- Propose deletion (in batches, queued for approval)
- Identify documents needing filing
- Propose filing structure (year/function/etc., in batches)
- Result: clean computer, everything on NAS, nothing lost

### 12c. Old hardware ingestion
- Apple AirPort backup drive (20 years of history)
- Samsung USB stick
- Read everything, dedupe against current NAS, file properly
- Result: 20 years of history searchable in one place

### 12d. Video processing
- Identify video files needing top-and-tail edits, title cards
- Queue them for Aaron's review
- Once approved, Winnie can use ffmpeg or similar to do the basic
  edits (top/tail trim, simple title cards) — anything complex Aaron
  does himself

This is **not Phase 0**. Don't worry about it now. Logged for when
core build is done.

## 13. Power resilience

**UPS connected to:** tower + monitor + NAS
**Battery window:** 15 minutes after power loss

**If Aaron is home when power fails:**
- Aaron decides whether to power down or wait

**If Aaron is NOT home when power fails:**
- Winnie detects UPS signal
- Stops accepting new actions
- Drains queue (executes any approved-but-pending actions)
- Writes "going dark" entry to audit log
- Sends Aaron a notification (Telegram/email): "Power lost, shutting
  down cleanly"
- Shuts down

**When power returns:**
- Tower + NAS boot
- Winnie boots, reads her queue, replays anything she missed
- Sends Aaron: "Back online. Here's what happened while I was out:
  X emails arrived, Y consultations had deadline changes, Z queue
  items expired."
